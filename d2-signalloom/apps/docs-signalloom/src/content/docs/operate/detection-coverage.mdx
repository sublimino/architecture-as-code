Signal Loom evaluates every observation from every collector against versioned
CEL policy, logs a finding per match, correlates findings across streams, and
stuns on a policy failure. This page is the operator map of that coverage.

## The three tiers

1. **Stage-1 rules** — every observation is evaluated against every tenant
   bundle's CEL rules; a match logs a `Finding`. A rule targets one stream (via
   `obs.source` + `obs.layer`) or several at once.
2. **Stage-2 windows** — a burst/threshold over one rule's findings, keyed by
   `[tenant_id, actor]`, produces a `Decision` which is routed to a stun.
3. **Correlation engine** — resolves one actor across layers and joins findings
   into attack-chains (exfil, account-takeover, privilege-cascade).

## Streams and what each detects

Every rule is source-gated (`obs.source`) and `has()`-guarded; identities are
matched as the pseudonymised `obs.actor` via a bundle `actor_vars` set, never a
cleartext label.

| Stream (layer) | Collector · enable with | Bundle | Detects (examples) |
|---|---|---|---|
| kube-apiserver audit (`kubernetes`) | `k8saudit-kube` · `COLLECTOR_K8SAUDIT_KUBE_MODE=live` (warden-local file/webhook, or EKS/GKE/AKS) | `k8s-audit.yaml` | pods/exec, secret read, RBAC escalation, token mint, privileged pod, CSR approve, webhook config, impersonate |
| cloud control plane (`cloud_control_plane`) | `awslogs` · `COLLECTOR_AWSLOGS_MODE` ; `gcpaudit` · `COLLECTOR_GCPAUDIT_MODE` | `cloud-signals.yaml`, `iam-change.yaml`, `gcp-admin.yaml` | IAM priv/key change, EKS/console/root, S3/GCS exposure, GuardDuty/logging-sink tamper |
| endpoint host (`endpoint_host`) | live `stun-sentinel` (in-kernel eBPF, source `stun-sentinel`) ; projection `collectors/ebpf` · `COLLECTOR_EBPF_ENDPOINT_MODE` (`fixture` \| `live-seam` \| `off`), source `sentinel.ebpf.syscall_trace` (provider `sentinel-ebpf`) ; `agent-nono` Landlock | `endpoint-runtime.yaml`, `landlock-denial.yaml` | sensitive-file open, unexpected execve, capability/ptrace (collector projection only today); Landlock denial |
| network egress (`network_egress`) | `netebpf` ; `awslogs` VPC flow | `network-egress.yaml` | direct-IP egress, off-cluster resolver, DNS tunnel (entropy bucket), VPC reject burst |
| identity & authz (`identity_authorization`) | `spirea` (SPIRE) ; SaaS login ; `baoaudit` | `identity-auth.yaml`, `openbao-audit.yaml` | SVID to an unexpected SPIFFE, foreign trust domain, impossible-travel/MFA-absent login, OpenBao root-token use |
| application SaaS (`application_saas`) | `workspacesaas` · `COLLECTOR_WORKSPACESAAS_MODE` | `application-saas.yaml` | mass download, external sensitive share, OAuth app grant, admin-console change |

The `behavioural` layer (evasive syscall burst) is detected in the stun-sentinel
itself, not via a CEL bundle.

### The two endpoint_host streams

`endpoint_host` carries two streams that share one label contract:

- **Live stun-sentinel** — in-kernel eBPF, no DaemonSet. Observations arrive with
  `source: "stun-sentinel"` and drive both Tier-0 in-kernel containment (cgroup
  freeze) and central CEL findings. Each observation carries `classification`
  (`file_access` or `process_exec`), `syscall`, `target`, and an `ActorHint`
  (`actor_hint`) that is pseudonymised into `obs.actor`.
- **Collector projection** — `collectors/ebpf`, selected with
  `COLLECTOR_EBPF_ENDPOINT_MODE` (`fixture` \| `live-seam` \| `off`); emits
  `source: "sentinel.ebpf.syscall_trace"` with provider label `sentinel-ebpf` and
  scopes the actor by service account.

`endpoint-runtime.yaml` source-gates its three rules — `sensitive-file-open`,
`unexpected-execve`, `capability-or-ptrace` — on **both**
`sentinel.ebpf.syscall_trace` and `stun-sentinel`. `landlock-denial.yaml` is the
separate `agent-nono`/Landlock endpoint stream, gated on `verification_status`.

Two residuals hold today:

1. The kernel probe cannot see a service account, so on the live `stun-sentinel`
   stream exec-scoping is by the workload **actor/pod pseudonym** — a trusted live
   workload is exempted by its actor identity in `allowed_exec_actors`, not by an
   SA name. The collector projection still scopes by service account.
2. `capability-or-ptrace` fires only on the collector projection today, because the
   sentinel does not yet probe ptrace/capability syscalls (planned).

An operator reads live sentinel endpoint findings on the dashboard **Signals**
page: filter the feed to the `endpoint_host` layer to see `stun-sentinel`
observations, their findings, and any resulting stun on the timeline. The feed's
"Filter Out Sentinel Events" toggle hides `stun-sentinel`-sourced rows, so leave
it off when inspecting live endpoint detections.

## Where an operator sees it

Live observations, findings and decisions stream to the dashboard **Signals**
page (`ObservationFeed` / `FindingsList` / `DecisionCard`, grouped by layer and
fidelity) over `/api/signals/observations` and its SSE stream; stuns appear on
the stun timeline. The `/coverage` API reports active layers and blind spots
(derived from the layer enum, so it never goes stale).

## Adding a stream

Each stream is one bundle + a collector projection into the shared label
contract (`store/label_allowlist.go` is the closed key set — add a key there,
never ship a label it refuses). Full method, label contract and the threat
catalogue: `docs/EAP/policy-detection-coverage-catalogue.md`. Every rule ships a
positive and a negative fixture asserted by a per-stream exact-count e2e table.
